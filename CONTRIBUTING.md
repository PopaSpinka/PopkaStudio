# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help! PopkaStudio is under the [MIT license](LICENSE): by sending a pull request you agree
that your code is published under it too.

- **Bugs and ideas** — open an [issue](https://github.com/PopaSpinka/PopkaStudio/issues): what you did, what you
  expected, what happened; a screenshot or the Load log helps a lot.
- **Pull requests** — welcome. Say what yours does and how you tested it: on which board, in the editor's preview, on
  the device itself, and what you couldn't try. If something changes for the boards that are already here, say that
  too.
- **Your board** — welcome as a pull request: its Halo port in `lib/Halo/src/ports/<board>/` and its passport in
  `boards/<board>/board.json`. Or open an issue with its name and a link, or write to lastmail.sk@gmail.com.
- **Not tested by me.** I may merge a pull request without trying it on real hardware — often I don't have that
  board. Such boards are listed in the README as **community boards**: tested by the people who added them, not by
  me. Questions about one go to its pull request or an issue, and a fix from anyone who has the board is welcome.

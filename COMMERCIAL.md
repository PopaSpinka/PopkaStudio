# Commercial use

PopkaStudio and the Halo library in this repository are **free for noncommercial use** under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Making money with them needs a **commercial license** from the
author.

## Free — no need to ask

- Your own watch: designing faces and apps for yourself, family and friends.
- Learning, teaching, school and university projects, hackathons, research.
- Hobby projects you share for free, videos and articles about them.
- Charities, schools, public research organizations and government institutions.

## Needs a commercial license

- Selling boards, watches or other devices running firmware made with PopkaStudio or Halo.
- Selling or licensing watch faces, apps or firmware built with them.
- Using them inside a company's product, service or paid work for a client.
- A paid online service or app built on PopkaStudio's code.

Not sure? Just ask — small makers and indie projects get a fair, simple deal.

## How to get one

Write to **lastmail.sk@gmail.com** with:

- who you are (a person or a company) and your country;
- what you are making and how many devices or users you expect;
- which parts you use (the editor, the Halo library, both).

## Board makers

Want your board supported by PopkaStudio and Halo — a driver, the editor's preview, and tuning for smooth 60+ fps?
Write to the same address.

## Good to know

- The weather comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com): free for noncommercial use only. A commercial product
  needs its own Open-Meteo plan (or another weather source).
- The older Halo v1.1 published in [PopaSpinka/HaloWatch](https://github.com/PopaSpinka/HaloWatch) stays under the
  MIT license; this repository's newer versions are under the license above.
